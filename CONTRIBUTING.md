# Contributions and copyright provenance

Please provide only geometry you have made yourself, and/or assets that you are authorized to redistribute under compatible explicit terms. Every addition must identify board manufacturer, exact PCB revision, measured size and fastener coordinates, source URL/version/license, required attribution, modifications, and accuracy/uncertainty.

Never submit commercial STL/STEP meshes, company branding, copied photos, modified vendor CAD, or schematic artwork without explicit and compatible permission. A public URL or an open hardware label is not on its own proof of permission to redistribute a file.

Label computationally estimated geometry as such. If a concrete IP complaint is received, review promptly and suspend disputed files while investigating; avoid falsely assuring that any AI-assisted geometry is infringement-free.
